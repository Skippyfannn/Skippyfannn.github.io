Discover Volleyball

Volleyball is a team sport where players send a ball over a net and try to make it land on the opponent's court.

6 Players

A standard indoor team has six players on court.

3 Touches

A team usually has up to three touches before returning the ball.

25 Points

Most sets are played to 25 points and must be won by two.
